# MGNREGA and rural FMCG: does MGNREGA predict what companies say about rural?

**Question.** Can MGNREGA data (rural job-scheme work), available before results, predict whether an FMCG company will describe rural demand as **accelerating, steady or slowing** on its next earnings call?

**Companies:** HUL and Dabur, Q1 FY23 to Q1 FY27 (17 quarters each).

## Results so far

Each test is scored on the most recent quarters, held out from rule fitting, and compared with the simple guess "same as last quarter".

| Test | MGNREGA rule | Repeat last quarter |
|---|---|---|
| HUL, overall rural market | 4 of 7 | **5 of 7** |
| Dabur, own rural business | 2 of 6 | **5 of 6** |
| Dabur, overall rural market | 1 of 7 | **6 of 7** |
| HUL, own rural business | not testable: HUL commented on its own rural trend in only 3 quarters | |

**What this means:** MGNREGA did not beat "no change" for either company. In 2023, rising MGNREGA work lined up with management describing rural as improving. From Aug 2025, MGNREGA persondays fell 25–54% year on year while both companies kept calling rural "steady". That fall looks like a scheme or policy change rather than a rural slowdown, so in the latest quarters the signal measures policy, not demand. The sample is small, so treat this as evidence, not proof.

Full numbers are in `outputs/results_<company>[_market].txt`. The charts are in `outputs/`.

## Quick start

Python 3.9+ with `pandas`, `numpy` and `matplotlib`:

```
pip install pandas numpy matplotlib

python run_all.py                                     # HUL, own rural (runs all steps)
python run_all.py --from 3 --target market            # HUL, overall rural market
python run_all.py --from 3 --company Dabur            # Dabur, own rural
python run_all.py --from 3 --company Dabur --target market
```

- `--from 3` skips the data-cleaning steps; use it once `data/clean/` exists.
- `--target market` predicts the company's comments on the overall rural market instead of its own rural business.
- `--company Dabur` switches company. The MGNREGA and rainfall data are shared.
- Outputs are named by company and target, e.g. `results_Dabur_market.txt` and `chart_accuracy_Dabur_market.png`, so runs don't overwrite each other.
- `python run_all.py --demo` runs everything on fake data to check your setup (writes to `*_demo` folders, which git ignores). **Never put demo numbers in the deck.**

## How the labels were made

The labels are **AI-generated drafts**. Three AI readers each read every transcript separately, plus Thurro filings and press coverage, without seeing each other's answers. The agreed label is the 2-of-3 majority.

| | HUL | Dabur |
|---|---|---|
| Reader 1 vs 2 agreement (own rural) | 94%, kappa 0.81 | 88%, kappa 0.80 |
| Quarters with a usable own-rural label | 3 | 16 |

Present these as AI labels with a human spot-check, not as human labels. Quarters worth checking by hand are those where the readers split, and those where the transcript couldn't be read. Both are flagged in the `label_source` column of each evidence sheet.

**Labelling rule:** compared with the previous quarter, did management describe rural as gaining momentum, similar, or losing momentum?
- Labels are Accelerating, Steady, Slowing or Insufficient.
- The **own** label counts only comments about the company's own rural business. Comments that are only about the market leave it **Insufficient**.
- The **market** label (`market_rural_label`) covers the company's comments on the overall rural market (Nielsen, demand, monsoon).

To change a label, edit `agreed_label` or `market_rural_label` in the evidence sheet, save it as CSV, and rerun with `--from 3`.

## How the model works (for the methods slide)

- **Prediction date:** the day before results (`results_date` in the evidence sheet).
- **MGNREGA lag:** month M is used only from M+3, because recent months get revised (Thurro guidance).
- **MGNREGA signals:** the change in year-on-year growth over the latest 3 usable months, for persondays and for a proxy wage bill (persondays × average wage). Year-on-year growth removes seasonality.
- **Rainfall signal:** the monsoon (Jun–Sep) departure from normal known on the prediction date, meaning this monsoon so far, or the last completed one. A plain 3-month window is mostly dry-season months, where percentage swings are noise. Rainfall data starts in Sep 2022, so the first full monsoon is 2023.
- **Rule:** above a cut-off → Accelerating, below a cut-off → Slowing, otherwise Steady. Both directions are tried, because more MGNREGA work could mean distress or income.
- **Fitting and testing:** cut-offs are chosen on the earliest 60% of labelled quarters, and the rule is scored on the rest. The "selected" rule is chosen among the MGNREGA rules, which is the project's question. Rainfall rules are shown for comparison.
- **Baselines** are scored on the same quarters:
  - repeat last quarter
  - follow the previous call's market commentary (own-rural test only)
  - the most common training label

## Caveats for the deck

- The wage bill is a **proxy**; there is no direct payment series.
- Persondays fell sharply from Aug 2025 (flagged in each results file), probably because of the scheme or policy change. Late quarters test the policy as much as rural demand.
- With 6–7 test quarters, one quarter changes the conclusion. Show counts, not just percentages.
- Training uses about 10 quarters, and only 6 of those have rainfall.

## Data sources

See `data/DATA_SOURCES.md`. MGNREGA and rainfall were pulled from Thurro (government MGNREGA MIS and IMD data). The evidence comes from company filings on NSE/BSE plus press coverage, with links in the evidence sheets.

## Original data-export prompts

**A. MGNREGA export → `data/raw/mgnrega_state_monthly.csv`**

> Export MGNREGA state-level monthly data from April 2019 to the latest month as ONE wide table with one row per state per month and these columns exactly: state, month_end (YYYY-MM-DD), persondays, households_worked, avg_wage_paid_inr, notified_wage_inr, persons_demanded. Persondays and households come from employment provided; persons_demanded from work demanded; wages are rupees per day. Keep missing values blank, do not fill them. Give a downloadable CSV, or batches by financial year if the table is too large.

**B. Rainfall export → `data/raw/rainfall_state_daily.csv`**

> Export IMD state-wise DAILY rainfall (single-day values, not month-to-date or year-to-date) for the full available history, one row per state per day, with columns exactly: state, date (YYYY-MM-DD), actual_mm, normal_mm. Keep missing values blank. Downloadable CSV, or monthly batches if too large.

If your exported column headers differ, **don't rename the CSV**. Edit `MGNREGA_COLUMNS` / `RAIN_COLUMNS` in `config.py`.

If you later want company-specific state weights, put them in `STATE_WEIGHTS` in `config.py`, label them as assumptions in the deck, and rerun with two or three alternative weightings.
