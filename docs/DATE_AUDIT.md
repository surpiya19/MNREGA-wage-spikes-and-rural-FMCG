# Transcript date corroboration

`data/raw/call_date_audit.csv` records an AI source audit of the 26 restored
Marico/Emami dates. The original A/B reader dates agree in every included quarter.
The cited PDFs were downloaded from the original company/exchange addresses and
their opening pages checked for the call date. SHA-256 fingerprints identify the
documents inspected. No full PDFs or transcript bodies are republished here.

- 24 input dates are corroborated as call dates in the transcript openings.
- Emami Q2 FY25 input date is 7 November 2024; the transcript says the call was
  8 November 2024. Emami Q3 FY25 input date is 27 January 2025; the transcript
  says the call was 28 January 2025. The earlier input dates are retained as a
  conservative cutoff. Whether they are the actual results-release dates remains
  to be checked; this is not represented as a verified release date.
- Two Marico URLs had an extra full stop after `.pdf` and before the page fragment.
  Removing that punctuation made both original company PDFs accessible. Only
  those source links were corrected; label decisions were preserved.

This is source corroboration performed with AI assistance, not independent human
agreement or a complete label-content audit. HUL/Dabur dates are outside this pass.
The earlier-deadline scenario also avoids depending on the exact late-quarter
results-versus-call convention, but it cannot prove exact historical data vintages.
