# MNREGA rural spikes – rural commentary labels

Labels of whether each company's management says its own rural business is accelerating, steady or slowing compared with the previous quarter. Covers Dabur, HUL, Marico and Emami. Labelled for Q1 FY24–Q2 FY27 from the Thurro filings corpus; the analysis uses Q1 FY24–Q1 FY27.

- `labelling_guide.md`: labelling rules (v2)
- `<company>/labels_A.csv`, `labels_B.csv`: the two independent labellers
- `<company>/labels_final.csv`, `reconciliation_report.md`: reconciled labels and notes
- `<company>/score_agreement.py`: agreement scoring (percent, Cohen's kappa, weighted kappa)
- `rural_labels_all_companies.csv`: all four companies combined
- `labelling_results_summary.md`: results and open items
