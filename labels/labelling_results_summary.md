# Rural commentary labels: results (Q2 FY24–Q2 FY27)

Each company was labelled by two independent agent labellers using guide v2. A third agent then reconciled each company's two sets against the sources.

## Agreement (13 scored quarters per company)
| Company | Agreement | Cohen's kappa | Final labels (13 scored) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dabur | 84.6% | 0.60 | 11 steady, 1 accelerating, 1 slowing |
| HUL | 92.3% | 0.72 | 11 blank, 2 steady |
| Marico | 76.9% | 0.20 | 11 blank, 2 steady |
| Emami | 76.9% | 0.65 | 6 blank, 5 steady, 2 accelerating |
| Pooled (52) | 82.7% | 0.71 | — |

## Key finding
HUL and Marico almost never describe their own rural business: in 11 of 13 quarters every rural remark is Nielsen or sector commentary. Dabur's own rural label is almost always steady. Only Emami has a direction label that changes from quarter to quarter. Labels about the companies' own rural business are therefore too sparse and flat to be the main backtest target on their own.
